<section class="reveal">
  <h2>Mission</h2>
  <p>
    We cultivate people who can shape humane futures through technology and design.
    Students are encouraged to ask better questions, prototype quickly, and create outcomes that matter in society.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="reveal">
  <h2>Five-Year Integrated Program</h2>
  <p>
    From the first year, students engage with domain knowledge and project-based learning.
    The curriculum gradually builds from foundations to advanced implementation and public presentation.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="reveal">
  <h2>Entrepreneurial Mindset</h2>
  <p>
    Through iterative experiments, users-first thinking, and teamwork, learners gain the ability to turn ideas into sustainable initiatives.
  </p>
</section>
