We held a one-day Community Hack Day with local residents and business partners in Kamiyama.

Teams worked on themes such as mobility, communication, and social connection.
By combining interviews and rapid prototyping, participants shared actionable proposals at the end of the day.

The outcomes will feed directly into next term's project-based courses.
